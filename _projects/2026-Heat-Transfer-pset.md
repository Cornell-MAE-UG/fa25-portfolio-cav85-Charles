The 3rd problem set of our heat transfer class focused on heat transfer analysis using the heat equation. The first problem involved deriving the full differential expression of the one-dimensional heat transfer of a cylindrical rod surrounded by ambient fluid. The second problem focused on applying the one-dimensional heat equation in cartesian coordinates to determine the temperature profile within a composite wall with one side being insulated. The last problem was centered around a slightly different topic with thermal resistance networks and understanding how to use them for modeling different applications such as a turbine blade. The problems focusing on using the heat equation improved my understanding on how conduction and convection influence temperature spatially across mediums. The problem focusing on the heat transfer analysis of turbine blades when adding an insulated layer showed how thermal resistance networks are useful for simplifying the calculation process of boundary temperatures. Both the heat equation and thermal resistance networks will be used in my project of designing a PID controller for temperature modulation.
